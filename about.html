MEET THE BAKER

Hello, I'm Minakshi Shah.
A baker by passion.

My love for baking has always been close to my heart. What started as a personal passion gradually turned into something truly special. On 10th February 2021, after the lockdown, I began my journey in the world of cakes and baking.

Based in Raipur Chakla, Ahmedabad Old City, I enjoy creating beautiful cakes with different flavours, styles and designs. I love experimenting with new varieties and bringing fresh ideas to every creation.

From customised celebration cakes to cookies and brownies, I enjoy making a wide variety of baked treats. Every order gives me an opportunity to learn, experiment and create something memorable.

Over the years, the love and trust of my customers have made this journey even more meaningful. Many customers return to me for their special occasions, and every new order motivates me to improve my work and explore new designs.

Even after five years of baking, my excitement for trying something new remains the same. I believe every cake should have its own personality, made with care, creativity and lots of love.

With love, Minakshi

BEFORE YOU PLACE AN ORDER

Terms & Conditions

01

50% Advance Payment

A 50% advance payment is required to confirm and reserve your cake order. The remaining balance must be paid as agreed when the order is completed.

02

Eggless Cakes

All cakes prepared at Minaxi Cake House are eggless.

03

Delivery Charges

Delivery charges will be calculated separately according to the customer's delivery location.

04

Cancellation Policy

Once the cake-making process has started, the order cannot be cancelled and the advance payment will not be refunded. However, the advance amount can be carried forward and reused for a future cake order.

05

Minimum Cake Weight

Cake orders start from 250 grams. Different varieties and types of cakes are available according to your requirements.

06

Custom Cake Pricing

Prices depend on the selected cake design, size, flavour, decoration and customisation. The final price will be shared before confirming your order.

A little request: Please place your cake order at least one day in advance. Customised designs may require additional preparation time.
