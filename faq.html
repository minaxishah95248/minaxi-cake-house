GOOD TO KNOW

A few sweet answers.

Some helpful details before you place your order.

How do I place a custom cake order?

Fill out our custom order form with your celebration details. It will open a WhatsApp message for you to review and send.

How far in advance should I order?

Advance notice is recommended, especially for detailed designs and larger celebrations. Contact us with your date to discuss availability.

Can I request a specific flavour or design?

Absolutely. Share your preferred flavour, theme, colours and inspiration in the custom order form.

How can I confirm the price?

Pricing depends on size, flavour and design details. Send us your requirements for a personalised quote.

Can I share an inspiration photo?

Yes. You can describe your inspiration in the form and share the image directly in the WhatsApp conversation.
