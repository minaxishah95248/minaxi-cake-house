MINAXI CAKE HOUSE — STATIC HTML/CSS/JAVASCRIPT WEBSITE

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract this ZIP folder.
2. Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser. No Node.js or npm is needed.
3. All pages are linked through the navigation and footer.

IMPORTANT BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Open js/site.js in Notepad or VS Code.
2. Replace YOUR_NUMBER_HERE with your WhatsApp number including country code, digits only (example: 919876543210).
3. Replace SITE_ADDRESS with the full shop address and pincode.
4. Replace the sample Unsplash images with your own cake photos when ready.
5. In contact.html, update the Google Maps iframe URL to your exact shop location.

PAGES
Home, About Us, Our Cakes (with category filters), Gallery, Custom Orders (WhatsApp enquiry form), Contact, FAQs.

NOTES
- The website uses Google Fonts and Unsplash image URLs, so an internet connection is needed for those assets.
- The WhatsApp form opens a pre-filled message; the customer reviews and sends it in WhatsApp.
- This is a static website. It can be hosted on Vercel, Netlify, or GitHub Pages without Node.js.
